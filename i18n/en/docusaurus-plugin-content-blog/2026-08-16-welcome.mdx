# Welcome to IPZ Docs

**IPZ Docs** is the official public knowledge, standards, and documentation portal of the IPZ ecosystem. It serves as a central hub for sharing knowledge assets gathered throughout research, product development, standard setting, and practical solution deployment.

{/* truncate */}

:::info
Core Principle
**Knowledge only holds value when it can be accessed and utilized.**  
Technology creates products. Research yields discoveries. But for these outcomes to generate sustainable value, they must be recorded, systematized, and widely shared.
:::

IPZ Docs transforms knowledge from fragmented results into structured, searchable, and continuously evolving documentation.

## 📚 Core Content Structure

IPZ Docs is organized into **4 primary content categories**:

* **🚀 Products & Tools:** User manuals, technical specifications, and operational documentation for applications and solutions within the IPZ ecosystem.
* **🛡️ IPZ Standards:** Public principles and regulations ensuring consistency, transparency, and inheritability throughout development.
* **🛠️ Guides & Practices:** Step-by-step procedures that help transform knowledge from *"knowing"* to *"doing"*.
* **🧬 Knowledge & Research:** Professional articles, in-depth analysis, and practical lessons learned.

---

## 🌐 Explore by Domain

The knowledge base is categorized into key domains actively researched and developed by IPZ:

* **Technology & Digital Security (`/blog/tags/cybersecurity`):** System security, information safety, and data protection solutions.
* **Intellectual Property (`/blog/tags/patent`):** Rights establishment, management, and value exploitation of IP assets.
* **Bioinformatics (`/blog/tags/bioinformatics`):** Molecular biology data analysis and computational methods.
* **Branding & Communications (`/blog/tags/brand`):** Building, positioning, and developing digital brand identity.

---

## 🔄 Three Layers of Knowledge & Development Lifecycle

Content on IPZ Docs operates across 3 interconnected layers of knowledge:

1. **Professional Insights:** Foundational knowledge and in-depth analysis.
2. **Research & Analysis:** Evaluations and new findings emerging from the R&D process.
3. **Practices & Experience:** Lessons đúc kết/learned from real-world implementations.

> **Continuous Knowledge Cycle:**  
> **Knowledge** ➔ **Research** ➔ **Practice** ➔ **Experience** ➔ **New Knowledge**

## 🏗️ IPZ Docs within the IPZ Ecosystem

The table below outlines the role of IPZ Docs in defining public scope and governance across the entire ecosystem:

| Component | Role & Governance Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IPZ** | Foundational platform and core brand of the entire ecosystem. |
| **IPZ Docs** | Delivers public knowledge, open standards, and documentation for the community. |
| **IPZ Products & Tools** | Embeds technology and knowledge into specific products, tools, and solutions. |
| **Specialized Systems** | Manages internal operational files, unreleased standards, and protected IP assets. |

:::note Publication Scope
Not all IPZ knowledge is publicly available on IPZ Docs. Published content is carefully curated and managed under principles of public scope, versioning, and document integrity.
:::
